Design system in one breath

  • 8-bit arcade, dark only. Two fonts: Press Start 2P (UPPERCASE chrome) + VT323 (body).
  • Color encodes meaning — player: John cyan / Griffin yellow / LJ pink; fate: keep green / move cyan / donate pink / trash red; reward: yellow.
  • Snappy, physical motion: buttons press inward, bars fill in discrete steps(), CRT scanlines over everything.
  • Family-friendly: only-ever-bonus mechanics, gentle losing. See ADR-0004.
